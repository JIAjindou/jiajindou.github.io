{%- comment -%} Records the visit as the very first thing on the page, before any CSS or JS is requested. Browsers hold a script until every stylesheet and script above it has downloaded; on slow links (some mainland China networks reach Cloudflare at ~10 KB/s) that took 30-40 s, and visitors who left sooner were never counted. Inline here, it runs as soon as the first few KB of HTML arrive. Include it at the top of on every page. ?debug in the URL shows an on-page panel listing every step, so a visitor on a device we can't access can screenshot exactly where recording fails. {%- endcomment -%}
